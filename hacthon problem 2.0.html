The Problem

Why is agricultural waste going unused?

🌾

Crop Waste Disposal

Farmers produce straw, rice husk, coconut waste and other crop residues. Without suitable buyers, some residue is burned or discarded.

🔎

Difficulty Finding Buyers

Farmers may not know which industries need their crop waste, what price to expect, or how to arrange transportation.

Our Solution

Making crop waste useful through a connected marketplace.

📝

Waste Listing

Record waste type, quantity, location and expected price through a simple listing.

🎯

Smart Buyer Matching

Identify potential buyers based on material type, distance and quantity.

🚚

Shared Transportation

Coordinate compatible loads from nearby farms to help reduce transportation costs.

📊

Price Guidance

Provide indicative price ranges using available market information and previous transactions.

📩

Basic Phone Support

Farmers without smartphones can register crop waste using a supported SMS format.

🔔

Status Updates

Receive listing references, buyer interest and collection updates through supported channels.

How SMS Registration Works

No mobile internet required for the proposed SMS workflow.

1

Send an SMS

The farmer sends crop waste details in the agreed format.

2

System Processes It

The service interprets the message and creates a listing.

3

Receive Confirmation

The farmer receives a reference number and next steps.
